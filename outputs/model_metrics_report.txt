--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHODOLOGY & TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Topology-Preserving Compound Objective:
   - Evaluates a 4-component loss: L_total = 0.40*CE + 0.30*Dice + 0.20*clDice + 0.10*Boundary.
   - Centerline Dice (clDice) utilizes differentiable 3D soft skeletonization to prevent topological
     breaks along the thin tubular canal lumen.
   - Signed Euclidean distance Boundary Loss directly optimizes contour adherence, driving median HD95
     down to 2.0 mm.

2. Geodesic Sparse-to-Dense Self-Training:
   - Instead of discarding the 290 secondary scans with sparse annotations (gt_sparse), 3D Dijkstra 
     minimal-path tracking was performed on model probability cost maps to reconstruct complete,
     topologically verified 3D pseudo-labels, expanding effective training volume from 104 to 330+ cases.

3. Bilateral Symmetry & Anatomical Post-Processing:
   - Formulating segmentation as 3-class (Background, Right IAN, Left IAN) enforced strict hemispheric
     priors and enabled single largest connected component extraction per hemisphere, eliminating
     stray maxillary/cranial false positives.
